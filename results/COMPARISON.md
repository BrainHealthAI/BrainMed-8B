# Cross-paper comparison status

Updated 20 October 2026. Published model scores and locally measured results
were not produced by a matched protocol. They are not a defensible leaderboard
or a test that BrainMed outperforms the published models.

The historical comparison is preserved in Git history. Its ranking and exclusion
of a published comparator are not part of the current conclusions.

The current [results report](REPORT.md) provides the measured base, all archived
candidate runs, the complete interpolation sweep, the repeat-evaluation
discrepancy and the published-protocol gap. [Published reference values](../eval/baselines.json)
remain unchanged for provenance, not as scientific acceptance criteria.

A matched comparison requires the same cases, prompts, extraction, decoding,
budgets and selection rules, with immutable model revisions and paired outputs.
